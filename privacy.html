CyberPrintsStudio Automation

Privacy policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

Application status: CyberPrintsStudio Automation is a private desktop application under development for the owner of CyberPrintsStudio. This notice describes its intended data handling. The application will be configured to follow this notice before it begins processing live Google data.

1. Who this notice covers

This notice covers CyberPrintsStudio Automation and this public information website. The desktop app is intended only for the shop owner, not for public registration or use by other sellers. Questions can be sent to cyberprints2023@gmail.com.

2. Information the app will handle

  • Local product materials: artwork selected by the owner, file names and paths, product theme details, generated previews, listing text, and delivery PDFs.
  • Authorized Google Drive information: product file contents being uploaded, file and folder identifiers, names, upload status, sharing permissions, and download or viewing links needed for product delivery.
  • Authorization credentials: Google OAuth access and refresh tokens used to connect the owner’s account, plus credentials required for the separately authorized Etsy and AI integrations.
  • Local operational records: job progress, product and listing identifiers, file mappings, and error information needed to prevent duplicate work and resume interrupted jobs.

Google Drive access will use https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file. This limits access to app-created files and folders and existing files explicitly authorized for this app. The app does not request broad access to all files in the owner’s Drive, Gmail, or Google Contacts.

3. Why information is used

Information will be used to organize and upload product bundles, create buyer PDFs containing the correct download links, prepare and publish the owner’s Etsy listings, and track processing results. Google access will be used for these disclosed product preparation and delivery functions.

4. Sharing and third-party services

Google Drive: Selected product files will be stored in the owner’s Google Drive. The owner’s configured workflow may make designated product folders viewable by anyone with their link. Anyone who receives or is forwarded a link can access that product content.

Etsy and buyers: Listing images, listing text, and buyer PDFs will be sent to Etsy. The PDFs will contain links to the corresponding product files in Drive. Google authorization credentials will not be included in listing materials or buyer downloads.

AI-assisted copy: Selected local product images and theme information may be sent to a separately configured AI service to generate listing text. Google authorization credentials and data fetched from Google Drive will not be sent to that AI service. The configured provider’s terms and privacy practices will apply to the local materials submitted to it.

Google account information and authorization credentials will not be sold, rented, shared with advertisers, or used for advertising profiles. Google API data will not be used to train general-purpose AI models. Sharing of product content will be limited to the owner-authorized workflow described above, except where disclosure is required by law.

5. Storage and protection

The desktop app is designed to keep job records and working files on the owner’s Windows computer and store uploaded product files in the owner’s Google Drive. Before live use, stored Google authorization tokens will be protected with local credential encryption, and service connections will use HTTPS. Credentials will be kept out of the public website, public source files, product downloads, and ordinary job logs.

This information website will not receive the desktop app’s credentials, private product files, or processing records.

6. Retention, deletion, and revoking access

The owner controls retention of local working files and job records. Product files in Google Drive will remain until the owner removes them or changes their sharing settings; they may be retained to keep purchased downloads available.

The owner can revoke the app’s Google access through Google Account connections and delete locally stored app credentials and records. Revoking authorization does not automatically delete uploaded Drive files or remove existing buyer links. Those files and sharing permissions must be managed separately in Google Drive.

Questions or deletion requests concerning information handled by the app can be sent to the contact address in this notice.

7. This website

This website contains static information pages. Its page code has no analytics scripts, advertising trackers, cookies, sign-in forms, or file upload forms. Hosting is provided by GitHub Pages, which may process technical request information, including IP addresses, under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

If you contact CyberPrintsStudio by email, your address and message will be used to handle the request.

8. Google API data commitments

Use and transfer of Google API data will follow the Google API Services User Data Policy, including applicable Limited Use requirements. Access will be restricted to the permissions needed for the functions described in this notice.

9. Changes and contact

This notice will be updated when the app’s data handling changes. Material changes affecting Google data will be disclosed, and any required new consent obtained, before the app uses data in the new way.
