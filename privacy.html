CyberPrintsStudio Automation

Privacy policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Application status: CyberPrintsStudio Automation is a private Windows application for the owner of CyberPrintsStudio. It includes Google Drive, Etsy and AI-assisted product preparation functions. Pinterest connection, review and publishing routines are implemented, Trial access is approved, and one separately authorized Sandbox test Pin has been verified. General Pinterest posting remains disabled. Section 8A describes current handling and unresolved historical-data retention.

1. Who this notice covers

This notice covers CyberPrintsStudio Automation and its public information website. The desktop app is intended only for the shop owner, not for public registration or use by other sellers. Questions can be sent to cyberprints2023@gmail.com.

2. Information the app handles

  • Local product materials: artwork selected by the owner; file names and paths; theme, folder and product details; generated previews; artwork analysis; listing text; and buyer delivery PDFs.
  • Authorized Google Drive information: account display name, email address and permission identifier used to verify the connected account; selected or app-created product files; file and folder identifiers, names, metadata, checksums, upload status, sharing permissions and download or viewing links. The app may retrieve file contents to verify or repair product delivery.
  • Etsy information: the connected shop's identifiers and details; listing text, images, digital files, prices, listing identifiers, status and publication results needed for the owner's listing workflow.
  • Authorization information: Google, Etsy and Pinterest OAuth credentials and tokens, configured AI-service credentials, and connection or authorization status.
  • Local operational records: artwork catalogs, approval decisions, file mappings and fingerprints, job progress, processing errors, listing and delivery records, AI outputs and usage records, and backups used to review work, avoid duplicate processing and resume interrupted jobs.

Google Drive access uses https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file. This restricts access to app-created files and folders and files explicitly authorized for the app, rather than broad access to every file in the owner's Drive. The app does not request Gmail or Google Contacts access. Pinterest materials, API information and connection handling are described in Section 8A.

3. Why information is used

Information is used to organize and prepare artwork, create previews and listing copy, upload product bundles, create buyer PDFs containing the correct download links, prepare and publish the owner's Etsy listings, and track processing results. Google access supports these product preparation and delivery functions.

Operational records support review, error recovery, duplicate prevention and the owner's management of the product library. Pinterest use is described separately in Section 8A.

4. Sharing and third-party services

Google Drive and product delivery

Selected product files are stored in the owner's Google Drive. The app's customer-delivery flow creates or verifies designated folders and files with Anyone with the link Viewer access. Someone who receives or is forwarded a working link can access the product content. Existing delivery-library or full-shop-bundle access can expose newly added content before its individual Etsy listing is published.

This describes the app's customer-delivery flow, not a promise that every production or working folder has the same permissions. Files placed in other owner-designated folders use those folders' existing sharing settings. The owner is responsible for choosing appropriate destinations and access.

Etsy and buyers

Listing images, text and buyer delivery PDFs are sent to Etsy for the authorized shop workflow. Buyer PDFs contain the relevant Google Drive product links. Google authorization credentials are not included in listings or product downloads.

AI-assisted preparation

Configured AI features send selected local artwork previews and relevant file names, folder or theme context, product facts and prompts to OpenAI or another separately configured AI service to analyze artwork and prepare product content. These are separate local-artwork inputs. Google authorization credentials and account or file data retrieved through the Google Drive API are not sent to the AI service.

The reviewed OpenAI request paths disable optional response storage using store:false. This setting is not a promise of zero provider retention. The provider's applicable terms and privacy practices govern its processing.

Other limits

Google account information and authorization credentials are not sold, rented, shared with advertisers or used to build advertising profiles. Google API data is not used to train general-purpose AI models. Product sharing is limited to the owner-authorized workflow, except where disclosure is required by law. Pinterest is addressed in Section 8A.

5. Storage and protection

The desktop application keeps working files, artwork catalogs, job records and backups on the owner's Windows computer. Uploaded product files are stored in the owner's Google Drive, and listing materials are sent to Etsy.

The app's saved Google and Etsy token records and its saved OpenAI API key use Windows-user-bound credential encryption. Resumable Google upload session information is also encrypted. This does not mean that all application files are encrypted: product materials, local databases, job records and backups may be ordinary local files, and a downloaded Google desktop-client configuration is kept separately.

Pinterest app credentials and tokens also use Windows-user-bound encryption; its separate environments and data handling are described in Section 8A.

External API connections use HTTPS. Local app interfaces listen on the computer's loopback address and use local access checks. These measures do not prevent access by all other software or people who have access to the Windows account or computer.

Credentials are kept out of public source files, product downloads and ordinary job logs. The public website does not serve as storage for the desktop app's product files or job records. Its Etsy connection callback handles temporary authorization values as explained in Section 7.

6. Retention, deletion and revoking access

The owner controls how long local artwork, working files, job records and backups are retained. There is no single automatic deletion schedule covering all application records. Removing an item from a working folder or catalog does not necessarily remove related history, receipts or backups.

Uploaded Google Drive product files remain until the owner removes them or changes their sharing. They may be retained so existing customers can use their purchased downloads. Published Etsy listings and materials remain subject to Etsy's controls and retention practices.

Some workflow-specific removal tools exist. Local artwork removal can use the Windows Recycle Bin while retaining an action record. A separately authorized discard workflow can remove a verified unpublished Etsy draft and move its matching Drive files to Trash. These are not universal account deletion or erasure features.

The owner can revoke Google access through Google Account connections and remove local credentials and records separately. Revoking access does not delete uploaded files, undo existing sharing or remove buyer download links. These need to be managed separately in the relevant service. Etsy connection access is managed through Etsy; local credential copies and retained records also need separate attention.

Questions or deletion requests can be sent to cyberprints2023@gmail.com. The operator can address records under its control; requests affecting Google, Etsy or another provider may also require that provider's controls. Pinterest retention and disconnect handling are described in Section 8A.

7. This website

The public site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Its information-page code has no analytics scripts, advertising trackers, sign-in forms or file upload forms, and does not set tracking cookies.

The site also contains an Etsy connection callback page. When the owner authorizes the desktop app on Etsy, Etsy redirects the browser to that page with a temporary authorization code and state value in the URL. The page reads those values, removes the query string from the displayed browser address using browser history replacement, and presents a response for the owner to copy into the desktop app. The desktop app then completes the token exchange.

The callback page has no external resources, browser-storage writes or outgoing network requests in its page code. The initial page request still reaches GitHub Pages, so removing the URL parameters in the browser does not establish that the hosting provider never received them or retained request information. The page does not receive the owner's Etsy password and does not itself create or publish listings.

GitHub may process technical request information, such as IP addresses, under the GitHub General Privacy Statement. This notice does not set GitHub's retention period.

Email addresses and messages sent to the contact address are used to handle those requests.

8. Google API commitments

Use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follow the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Google API access is restricted to permissions necessary for the disclosed product preparation and delivery functions. Material changes to Google data use will be disclosed and any required new consent obtained before the changed use begins.

8A. Pinterest connection, review and data handling

The app prepares organic Pin candidates from the owner's artwork and Etsy product materials in a separate review queue that does not stop Etsy processing or archiving. The owner previews and chooses the image, reviews or edits the title, description, alternative text and Etsy link, and chooses an owned board and optional section. Publishing requires specific owner authorization and a live Etsy listing. Preparation alone does not publish anything. Trial access is approved and one separately authorized Sandbox test Pin has been verified; Standard access has not been granted and general posting remains disabled.

Connection uses Pinterest OAuth, not collection of Pinterest passwords or browser session cookies. The current permissions cover account reading, board reading and writing, and Pin reading and writing. App credentials and access and refresh tokens are encrypted locally for the Windows user. Production and Sandbox use separate token records and API environments.

The app reads account, board, section and Pin information to verify destinations and results. An authorized submission sends Pinterest the selected image, text, Etsy link and destination identifiers. The integration does not buy advertising or automate likes, comments or follows. Pinterest information is not sold, shared with advertisers or sent to an AI provider. Pinterest handles information received by its service under its own Privacy Policy.

New operational records retain local artwork, authored product text, Etsy links, local source and candidate identities, owner-action records and submission-attempt markers. Pinterest destination identifiers and labels, returned Pin identifiers, verification results and API diagnostics remain in memory for the current session rather than being added to these records. Restart or reconnection requires fresh destination selection for unsubmitted candidates. A durable local marker is written before submission and remains afterward to prevent automatic duplicate attempts, including when the result is uncertain. After restart, an attempted Pin remains held rather than restoring a saved Pinterest URL or confirmed result.

Historical development records, evidence files and backups still include Pinterest identifiers, earlier destination labels and fingerprints, and complete Sandbox test responses. These have not been deleted or migrated. No automatic deletion schedule covers them, and the permitted scope and duration of their retention remain unresolved. The newer handling does not erase this historical information or establish Pinterest approval of its retention. An earlier uncertain submission remains held against automatic retry.

Disconnect removes active access and refresh tokens for the selected environment, but retains its app ID and app secret. It does not erase local artwork, owner-action records, attempt markers, historical evidence or backups; revoke authorization through Pinterest; or delete existing Pins. Pinterest-side access and content must be managed separately through Pinterest. Questions or requests about locally retained information can be sent to cyberprints2023@gmail.com.

9. Changes and contact

This notice will be updated when data handling changes. Material changes and any required consent will be addressed before the changed use begins. Operator: CyberPrintsStudio. Contact: cyberprints2023@gmail.com.
