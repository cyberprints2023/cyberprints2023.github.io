App information

CyberPrintsStudio
Automation

A private Windows workflow for preparing digital pattern listings and delivering product files through Google Drive.

In development · For the shop owner’s use

From artwork to a prepared listing.

The application is being developed for the owner of CyberPrintsStudio to manage the shop’s own digital products. The owner supplies and selects the artwork that enters the workflow.

01 / PREPARE

Prepare listing materials

Arrange product previews, generate listing copy from selected local materials, and prepare listing details for the owner’s Etsy shop.

02 / ORGANIZE

Upload product files

Create product folders in the owner’s Google Drive, upload selected pattern images and archives, and keep track of their file identifiers and links.

03 / DELIVER

Create buyer downloads

Prepare a PDF containing the relevant product download link, then upload the PDF and listing materials to Etsy under the owner’s configured publishing rules.

Why Google Drive access is requested

The desktop app needs permission to create and organize the product files the owner chooses to sell. It will also read the file metadata needed to confirm uploads and create the correct buyer links.

Its Google permission is limited to drive.file: files and folders created by the app, plus any existing files the owner explicitly authorizes for the app. It does not request permission to browse the owner’s entire Drive.

Uploads and sharing follow predefined program rules. AI-assisted listing copy will use selected local artwork and theme details; Google Drive access is handled by the desktop program.

How buyer links work

When the owner enables delivery for a product, its designated Drive folder will be shared with view-only access for anyone who has the link.

Anyone who obtains that link can access the shared product files. Only designated product content is intended for this sharing.

A private tool for one shop

The application is intended for CyberPrintsStudio’s owner only. It is not offered as a public service, and this website has no app registration or Google sign-in form.

Development is ongoing. The published privacy notice describes the intended data handling and will be updated before any material change in how Google data is used.
